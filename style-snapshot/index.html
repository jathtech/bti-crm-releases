About this assessment

This is a forced-choice (ipsative) assessment. Every set of four words takes exactly the ranks 4, 3, 2 and 1, so your four scores always total 100 and each dimension can range from 10 to 40. The scores describe how strongly the four tendencies compare with one another within you. They are not percentiles, not a comparison with other people, not a measure of ability or psychological health, and not a diagnosis.
