Moving the slider sets the throttle from the playhead onward and re-flies the rest of the mission instantly; the faint dotted line in the flight view is where you are now headed. PLAN hands control back to the recorded schedule.

Each engine's moment about the centre of mass is shown: an outboard engine with no partner is what rolls the ship. Shutting one down is safe; relighting is a roll of the dice (centre 85%, RVac 70%) and a failed relight is permanent. Spool-up takes 1.6 s.

Drag left or right to swivel the three centre engines (up to ±10° of trim). The flame points where the engines point; the nose swings the same way. Red arc: moment from the engine imbalance. Green arc: moment from the gimbal. White: what is left over. The SAS adds up to ±3° on top of your trim to hold the plan; when it shows SAT it has run out.
