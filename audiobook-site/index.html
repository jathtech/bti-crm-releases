The pipeline

Six passes, in order, every time.

Each stage hands a finished artifact to the next, so you can stop after any one of them and pick the file up in your own tools.

  1. 01

    Import the manuscript

    DOCX, EPUB, PDF or plain text. Front matter, page numbers and running headers are stripped; footnotes are held aside for you to place or drop.

    Produces a clean reading script

  2. 02

    Detect chapters and dialogue

    Headings become chapter breaks with their own markers. Quoted speech is attributed to the character who said it, and you correct anything it got wrong in a single pass.

    Produces a marked-up script with speaker tags

  3. 03

    Cast the voices

    Assign a narrator, then a distinct voice per speaking character. Audition any line before you commit, and lock a voice so a re-render never reassigns it.

    Produces a cast sheet bound to the script

  4. 04

    Render the takes

    Chapters render in the background while you keep editing. Pronunciation fixes apply per book, so a character's name is never mispronounced twice.

    Produces raw takes at 44.1 kHz

  5. 05

    Master to spec

    Levelling, de-essing, room tone and head/tail trim run per chapter, then each file is measured against the retail targets above and flagged if it misses.

    Produces mastered, measured chapters

  6. 06

    Export the package

    Per-chapter MP3s for retail upload, a chaptered M4B for your own library, plus the opening credits, closing credits and a retail sample cut to length.

    Produces an upload-ready folder

Questions

Before you download.

Will retailers accept a synthetically narrated audiobook?
Policies differ by retailer and several now require you to disclose synthetic narration at upload. The export includes the technical package retailers check against; confirming the disclosure rules for the store you're selling through is your call, and they change often.
Do I keep the rights to the audio?
You own the recordings you generate and can sell them anywhere, on any plan, including after a subscription lapses.
How long does a full-length book take to render?
A 90,000-word manuscript renders in roughly two to three hours on a recent machine, in the background, and you can keep editing later chapters while earlier ones finish.
Can I fix a name the voice keeps mispronouncing?
Yes — add it once to the book's pronunciation dictionary and every chapter picks up the correction on the next render. Dictionaries carry across a series.
Does my manuscript get uploaded anywhere?
Rendering runs locally on your own machine. Your manuscript and finished audio stay in the project folder you choose.
